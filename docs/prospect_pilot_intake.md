# Founding pilot intake and delivery checklist

**Use:** assisted customer conversations while the 15/week hypothesis is being tested. Do not represent this as a self-serve volume guarantee. Apply the [review rubric](prospect_pilot_rubric.md) to every delivered card.

## Qualify the customer before promising delivery

Record one approved targeting brief per workspace:

1. **Offer:** product/service URL if available, one-sentence value proposition, price range, sales cycle and what an ideal first conversation looks like. The website is an optional source for drafting, not the authority on the customer's target.
2. **Buyer:** industries, company sizes/stages, geography, language, required technologies or workflows, buyer roles and budget owner if the customer knows them. Mark every criterion as required or preferred.
3. **Exclusions:** current customers, active opportunities, do-not-contact accounts, competitors, geographies, regulated/sensitive categories and any named bad-fit examples.
4. **Examples:** five accounts they would contact and five they would reject, with the reason for each. Ask for their last ten actual outbound targets when available; these reveal the real standard.
5. **Action route:** acceptable channels (public discussion reply, business contact page, customer-owned CRM record, licensed business contact lookup), customer CRM import permission and who will perform outreach. Arcli does not send outreach in this pilot.
6. **Success:** their weekly outreach capacity, present prospecting time and spend, minimum useful weekly volume, what makes a prospect worth contacting, and how they will return feedback. Ask whether they would renew at the proposed pilot price if the feed works.

Check coverage before accepting a paid pilot: at least two approved source paths, a plausible pool for four weeks, contact routes, reviewer capacity and a source-rights/retention decision. If coverage is weak, explain that finding and do not enroll the workspace in a 15/week experiment.

## Pilot offer language to adapt after pricing is set

> Arcli's founding pilot delivers a manually reviewed feed of companies or public discussions that fit your approved target. Each recommendation shows its evidence, an outreach angle and a usable contact or reply route. Some have direct buying signals; others are high-fit prospects without a recent signal. We are testing whether we can consistently reach 15 worthwhile recommendations per week in your market. We will report actual volume and quality each week and agree on a remedy if coverage falls short.

Set the exact cohort size, price, dates, delivery channel, cancellation/credit terms, data-use terms and named owner before using this wording in checkout or a sales agreement. Do not leave “remedy” undefined in a paid offer.

## Daily operator procedure

1. Confirm the active brief revision and exclusions. Import the customer's already-known accounts only through an agreed, tenant-scoped method.
2. Search approved sources; record provider requests, hits, unique entities, source rights and cost. Keep direct public conversations separate from company-fit research.
3. Dedupe by canonical entity and delivery history. Verify fit facts, source links, event dates if any, and action route. Write a short card with a defensible angle and explicit uncertainty.
4. Review against every required rubric check. Double-review the initial sample and every direct-intent claim. Deliver only approved cards; a short day is a real finding.
5. Ask the customer for explicit verdicts on delivered cards. Capture contacted and meeting outcomes where they agree to share them. Record each rejection reason for query/source and brief calibration.
6. At week's end, send delivered and accepted counts by tier, contact actions, source shortfalls, duplicates, broken evidence and the next sourcing adjustment. Compare cost and review time with the pilot price.

## Pilot decision at four weeks

- **Expand this segment:** each eligible workspace received 15 valid recommendations in 4/4 weeks, at least 12/15 were explicitly worth contacting, customers acted on enough cards to justify renewal, and unit economics work at the proposed price.
- **Continue assisted and repair:** customer value is clear, but one source, contact route or reliability bottleneck caused a measured shortfall; state the issue, owner and retest date.
- **Change segment or offer:** repeated source exhaustion, low customer acceptance or weak willingness to pay persists after rubric and source corrections. Do not lower the bar or rename high-fit accounts as intent leads.
